IGP is a global publishing house dedicated to reviving, translating, & beautifully producing works of scholarship. Since 2007. Now in Malaysia as IGP MY.

Since 2007

Since 2007, we have led the effort to revive the vast and rich Islamic scholarly tradition. The Islamic tradition is a deep and vast ocean of jewels: works lost for centuries are found, translated and printed with ihsan.

IGP MY brings these books to Malaysia, with prices in RM, a Bahasa Melayu shelf and classes in Kuala Lumpur. .

How a book is made

  1. The manuscript

    The Maghazi manuscripts were found in the National Library of Tunisia by Dr Muhammad Tabarani, lost for over 400 years.

  2. The translation

    Arabic beside English, “set in clear Arabic script with accompanying” translation and transliteration. Mukhtasar Sahih al-Bukhari appears in English for the first time in history.

  3. The making

    “Excellence takes time. Seven years, and every page done with ihsan.” Paperback, hardback, gold-illuminated and leather editions.
