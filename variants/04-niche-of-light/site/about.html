Reviving the tradition, since 2007

IGP is a global publishing house dedicated to reviving, translating, & beautifully producing works of scholarship. IGP MY brings these books to Malaysia.

A deep and vast ocean of jewels

Since 2007, we have led the effort to revive the vast and rich Islamic scholarly tradition. Works lost for centuries are found, translated and printed with ihsan.

IGP MY brings them home to Malaysia: prices in RM, a Bahasa Melayu shelf, nine free ebooks and classes in Kuala Lumpur. Nurturing a new generation of readers.

How a book is made

Three steps, lit one at a time.

  1. The manuscript

    The Maghazi manuscripts were found in the National Library of Tunisia by Dr Muhammad Tabarani, unseen for over 400 years.

  2. The translation

    Arabic beside English, set in clear Arabic script. Some works, like Mukhtasar Sahih al-Bukhari, appear in English for the first time in history.

  3. The making

    Paperback, hardback, gold-illuminated Gold Premium and leather editions, printed, packed and sent to readers.
